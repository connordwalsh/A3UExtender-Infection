# Antistasi Ultimate × Zombies and Creatures Compatibility Extension

## Project Mission Statement and Scope

### Mission

This project will make **Antistasi Ultimate** and **WebKnight’s Zombies and Creatures** work together as one coherent Arma 3 campaign.

The goal is not simply to make the two mods load at the same time. The project should adapt Antistasi’s factions, missions, enemy forces, and spawning behavior so that the zombie outbreak is a functioning part of the campaign. Players should encounter a world where human factions and infected forces interact in ways that are understandable, consistent, and playable from the beginning of a campaign through its later stages.

The project will provide a dedicated infected Occupant faction, adjust existing missions where needed, add or replace missions that make sense in an outbreak setting, and establish rules for how ordinary infected and special infected appear. These changes should make the zombie threat feel integrated into Antistasi rather than like an unrelated group of enemies added on top.

## What the Project Needs to Do

### 1. Connect the two mods into one campaign

The project should identify and address the places where Antistasi Ultimate expects human factions, soldiers, vehicles, garrisons, patrols, and mission targets. It should adapt those parts so Zombies and Creatures can fill the intended roles without breaking the campaign’s normal flow.

Players should be able to start and play an Antistasi campaign with the compatibility extension enabled. The extension should provide the configuration and mission changes required for the two mods to operate together. Any required dependencies should be clearly identified.

### 2. Create an infected Occupant faction

The project should define an Occupant faction representing the infected presence in the campaign. It should specify which zombie units belong to that faction and how they are used in the world, including where appropriate:

- Units used for towns, military locations, patrols, or mission sites.
- Basic infected based on ordinary human units.
- Special infected with distinct abilities or combat roles.
- Any faction equipment, vehicles, or other assets required by Antistasi’s systems.

The faction should be configured so Antistasi can use it consistently wherever it expects the Occupant forces to appear. Its composition should fit the intended difficulty and setting rather than being a random selection of available zombie units.

### 3. Adapt missions to the outbreak

The project should review Antistasi’s existing missions and determine which ones work as written, which need changes, and which do not fit the campaign.

Missions that depend on ordinary human enemies may need their targets, defenders, patrols, or mission conditions adjusted to account for infected forces. The project should also consider missions that create meaningful interactions among the resistance, human factions, civilians, and infected. Where existing missions cannot support the intended experience, the project may add replacement or new missions that fit the outbreak.

Mission changes should preserve clear objectives and reliable completion conditions. Players should be able to understand what they need to do and recognize when they have succeeded, even when infected units are involved.

### 4. Define how infected spawn

The project should establish where and under what conditions infected units appear. This includes adapting Antistasi’s spawning behavior so that the infected faction is used in the appropriate locations and situations.

The spawning rules should distinguish between basic infected and special infected. Basic infected may be used as the more common threat, while special infected should appear according to deliberate rules such as location, mission, difficulty, or other campaign conditions. Special units should not appear unpredictably in ways that make the campaign unfair or undermine its pacing.

The project should also account for how infected spawns interact with Antistasi’s existing systems for garrisons, patrols, reinforcements, and mission forces, as applicable.

### 5. Make the experience understandable and playable

The resulting campaign should communicate its rules through gameplay. Players should be able to recognize the infected threat, understand the role of different enemy types, and encounter them in ways that feel consistent across missions and locations.

The extension should avoid situations where the campaign behaves as though the Occupants are a normal human army when they are meant to be infected, or where mission logic depends on behaviors the zombie units cannot provide. Difficulty and unit placement should support a playable campaign rather than relying on sheer numbers or surprise alone.

### 6. Keep the extension maintainable

The project should keep its compatibility work identifiable and organized so it can be reviewed, updated, and adjusted as either source mod changes. Its documentation should explain what the extension changes, what it requires, and any known limitations.

The project should make its intended behavior explicit, especially for faction composition, mission changes, and infected spawning. This will make it easier to tell whether the campaign is behaving as designed and to revise individual systems without losing sight of the overall goal.

## Definition of Success

The project will have achieved its mission when players can run an Antistasi Ultimate campaign with Zombies and Creatures in which:

- The infected Occupant faction is recognized and used by Antistasi’s relevant campaign systems.
- Missions that involve Occupant forces work with the infected faction, and unsuitable missions have been adapted or replaced where needed.
- Infected units appear through clear, intentional spawning rules.
- Basic and special infected have distinct, purposeful roles.
- The campaign remains coherent and playable across its major systems, rather than merely loading both mods at once.
- The project’s requirements, behavior, and known limitations are documented clearly.
